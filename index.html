🛡️ Electronic Warfare: Passive Bistatic Space Radar (GRAVES & BRAMS)

The French GRAVES radar (Grand Réseau Adapté à la Veille Spatiale) transmits a 143.050 MHz CW beam upward. Receiving stations like CAMRAS in Dwingeloo intercept reflections off space targets passively—detecting satellites, ballistic launches, and meteor trails with zero RF emissions of their own.
